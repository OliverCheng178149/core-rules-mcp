# Synthetic Clockwork Rules

## PDF Page 1

### Gear Fatigue
Every clockwork creature or device tracks gear fatigue after each long rest.
When a device works for more than eight hours, its keeper rolls a d6 for wear.
On a roll of 1, the device gains one level of gear fatigue until it is oiled.
A device with three levels stops moving until someone spends an hour winding it.
Winding a device removes one level of gear fatigue and spends one key charge.

### Lantern Spark
Level 1 Evocation
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S, M (a scrap of copper wire)
Duration: 1 minute
A mote of amber light leaps from your hand to a point you can see within range.
The mote sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for another 10 feet.
A creature that ends its turn within 5 feet of the mote takes 1d6 fire damage.

## PDF Page 3

### Clockwork Compass
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
This palm-sized compass has a needle of polished brass and blue enamel.
While holding it, you can use an action to learn the direction of the nearest gear.
The compass ticks softly whenever a clockwork creature is within 60 feet of you.

SPRING TENSION                                            OILING SCHEDULE
A wound spring stores effort for later use.               Oil each joint at dawn and again at dusk.
Tension rises by one step each hour of work.              Skipped oilings add one step of gear fatigue.
At four steps the spring must be released.                A full flask covers ten joints of a warden.
Releasing tension takes ten quiet minutes
and returns the spring to its resting step
without harming the device that holds it.
                                                          Record every oiling in the maintenance log
                                                          and note which joints squeaked that day
                                                          so the next keeper knows where to begin.
